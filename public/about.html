What can you do here?

Quiet Numbers helps you track your money in a clean and simple way.

  • Home: See totals (Income / Expense / Balance) and your transactions list.
  • Add: Add a new income or expense with a category and amount.
  • Theme: Switch light/dark mode (saved automatically).

Tip: Add a few transactions, then return to Home to see the totals update.
